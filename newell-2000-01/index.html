Honors

  • West River Invitational All-Tournament Team Luke Bonefield, Josh Wilson, Chester Ismay Newell won the tournament, and Bonefield was its MVP. Rapid City Journal, January 14, 2001, page 43.
  • State Class A All-Tournament Team Luke Bonefield, Craig Kirk Two of the twelve players named, after Newell's run from a No. 7 seed to second place. Argus-Leader, March 19, 2001, page 17.
  • All-Black Hills Conference Luke Bonefield (senior), Chester Ismay (senior), Josh Wilson (junior) Newell, third in the league, and champion St. Thomas More each had three players on the team. Rapid City Journal, March 20, 2001, page 21.
  • Academic All-State, Class A Shawn Shelley, Chester Ismay Chosen by the South Dakota Basketball Coaches Association. Argus-Leader, April 4, 2001, page 18.

Sources

The videos are VHS tapes copied to DVD and then to digital files, split into one file per game. The camcorder clock was not always right, so dates come from the newspaper, not the tape.

  • Scores and dates: the Rapid City Journal and the Argus-Leader, December 2000 to April 2001, read on Newspapers.com.
  • State tournament bracket: the SDHSAA 2000-01 yearbook.
  • The team's 2021 induction into the Newell High School Sports Hall of Fame: KBHB News.

Two scores need a note. The Lemmon headline printed 65-51, but the box score adds up to 61-55. The Timber Lake score ran only in statewide scores lists. The Rapid City Journal's copy is blurry, but the Argus-Leader's reads 78-62.

Put together by Chester Ismay, a senior on the 2000-01 team.
