How this was worked out

Teams with the biggest sway

Sway is how far the chances of who wins move between that team going Over and going Under (0 means nobody's chances change). Teams where everyone picked the same side barely matter, even with big wagers. Cells show each pick and its wager.

Rooting guide

For each team, the side that raises this player's chance of winning the pool, and by how many percentage points. Usually that is the player's own pick, but not always: when rivals bet more on the same side, the team hitting helps them more.

How similar the picks are
